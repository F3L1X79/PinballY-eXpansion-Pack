# Achievements and Challenges

[← Guide index](index.md)

## Weekly Challenges

<img src="../../images/challenges.png" alt="The week's Challenge Card with its progress and the days left" width="368">

A new Challenge comes every Monday, the same for the whole household: five different Stern tables, three tables you have never played, half an hour on a single table...

- Its card on the wheel screen tracks your progress. A game on a hidden or unconfigured table does not count.
- **Challenge Tables**, right under "All Tables" behind "Change Filter" in the main menu, keeps only the tables that would move it forward, and leaves the list once there are none.
- The Welcome Screen also shows one of your Challenge Tables (see [Choosing a table](choosing_a_table.md)).
- Completing it brings a shower of confetti across the wheel screen.

## Achievements

<img src="../../images/achievements.png" alt="The Achievement List" width="380">

There are dozens of Achievements, from Bronze to Platinum: manufacturers and decades completed, hours played, Streaks, Challenges, High Scores, a full tour of the wheel...

<img src="../../images/toasts.png" alt="An Achievement Toast" width="440">

- Each one is announced by a small toast in a corner, without interrupting your games; a Platinum also brings a shower of confetti across the wheel screen.
- The Achievement List, opened from **My Achievements** in the main menu, shows how far you are from each missing one, and which of the household's Avatars already have it.
- Completing the collection, a manufacturer, a System, a decade or a category leaves out hidden and unconfigured tables, so one can unlock when its last unplayed table leaves the list.
- The System Achievements ("Pinball FX Master" for every Pinball FX table played, say) exist only when the tables you can see use two Systems or more, named as in PinballY's settings. On a cabinet with a single System, there are none. With them comes World Walker, for one table played on each System: when a new System with tables arrives, it is missing again until you play one of its tables, and it comes back without a second toast.
- A category needs at least two tables you can see to have its Achievement. When a category drops to one table (a table hidden, or its category removed), its Achievement goes, even if you had it, and its points leave your Player Level.
- A few stay secret, shown as "???" with a hint, until you stumble upon them.
- The Ratings family rewards rating tables: First Verdict for 1 table rated, Armchair Critic for 5, Grand Inquisitor for 10, Oracle of the Playfield for 25. Every Rating you gave counts, a 0-star one too, even for a table no longer on the cabinet; removing a Rating can take a step away again. A step shows as soon as you close the rating dialog, whether you opened it from the main menu or from the prompt after a game. It exists only while Ratings per Profile is on (see [Settings](settings.md)).
- A Child Profile has three more of its own: Snack Time, Wednesday Off and Beat the Grown-Ups, a Table Record taken from a grown-up.

The toast's time on screen and size, the sound, and the confetti can be changed in [Settings](settings.md).

## Player Level

Each Achievement earns points toward your **Player Level**, the more the higher its Rank, with no last level. It shows as a small pip on your Avatar, in bronze, then silver, gold and platinum as you climb, and reaching a new level brings its own toast and fireworks.
