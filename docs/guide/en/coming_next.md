# Coming next

[← Guide index](index.md)

Here is what we are thinking about for the pack's future. Nothing is promised: plans change, and some of these ideas may never ship.

- **Rocket Game on the backglass**: during the Rocket Game, the backglass screen becomes the game's marquee, with the score and the Rocket Board, in place of the table's backglass.
- **A cabinet that shakes**: a big moment sets off the cabinet's DOF toys, such as the shaker or the flashers.
- **Every screen together**: a comet that falls from the topper to the DMD, then to the backglass, and bursts on the wheel to celebrate a record.
- **Year in Pinball**: each Profile's year in review, the tables played and discovered, the hours, the favourites, the Achievements and the longest Streak.
- **Themes**: other looks than Steamball for every drawn screen, with a small editor to make your own.
